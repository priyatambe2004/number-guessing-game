Questions

Do invalid guesses cost an attempt?

No. Empty, decimal, out-of-range and repeated guesses show a message and are not counted.

How is the hot-or-cold reading worked out?

It compares how far your guess is from the answer with the size of the range. Closer than 3% of the range is burning hot.

What does the hint do?

It tells you whether the secret number is odd or even. You can use it once per game and it uses one attempt.
